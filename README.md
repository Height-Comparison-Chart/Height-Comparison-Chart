# Height Comparison Chart

**Free, true-to-scale height and size comparison tools — directly in your browser.**

[Try the online chart maker](https://heightcomparisonchart.site/) · [Compare two heights](https://heightcomparisonchart.site/height-comparison) · [Calculate a height difference](https://heightcomparisonchart.site/height-difference-calculator)

Height Comparison Chart helps you see real-world differences instead of just reading measurements. Put people, animals, everyday objects, or original characters side by side on a shared scale. Adjust their dimensions, see the result instantly, and share or download your comparison.

No sign-up required. The online tools are free to use.

## What you can do

- **Build a height comparison chart.** Compare up to 12 subjects on one scale, with custom names, colors, and measurements.
- **Visualize height differences.** See who's taller, by how much, and how the difference looks in proportion.
- **Compare more than people.** Explore animal and object measurements, including height, length, and width where data is available.
- **Compare TV and monitor sizes.** See two screens at their correct relative size using diagonal measurements and aspect ratios.
- **Create character lineups.** Give original characters their own heights and silhouette styles for writing, art, and worldbuilding.
- **Share your result.** Copy a link to your comparison or export a PNG; SVG export is available in supported chart tools.

## How it works

1. **Choose a comparison tool** based on what you want to measure.
2. **Enter heights or dimensions**, or start with available presets.

## A note on accuracy and privacy

All objects in a comparison are plotted using a consistent numerical scale. The silhouettes are **illustrations**, not anatomically precise representations. Preset measurements may have different definitions (for example, an animal's shoulder height versus its total height), so check the measurement notes when comparing different kinds of subjects.

The height chart maker runs in your browser without requiring an account. Its documented sharing flow encodes the chart setup in the link itself rather than uploading your chart to a server.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Height Comparison Chart free?**  
Yes. You can create and export charts with no sign-up.

**Can I compare more than two people?**  
Yes. The main chart maker supports up to 12 subjects; the dedicated character lineup supports up to six.

**Can I download the image?**  
Yes. PNG export is available, and most chart tools also support SVG export.

**Is the visual the exact shape of a person or animal?**  
No. The illustrative silhouettes communicate measured scale, not anatomical detail.
